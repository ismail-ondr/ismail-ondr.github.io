Strait Control — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Strait Control is a mobile game for Android in which you draw routes for ships in busy straits. This policy explains what information is processed when you play it, by whom and why. The game is developed by an independent developer (“we”). Contact: ismailonder.dev@gmail.com.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how the game is played through Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services process device and usage data as described below.
  • We do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, rank, best scores, sound and language settings and a small counter of how many times you tried each level are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: an optional rewarded video that lets you rewind after a crash, and occasional full-screen ads between games. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form when you first open it. Ads are personalised only if you agree; otherwise non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad choices. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads).

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to improve the game, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It receives:

  • game events: a level or endless run started or ended, its result (won, crashed, quit, restarted), score, stars, duration, how close you got to the goal, which kinds of ships crashed, whether you used the rewind, tutorial start and end, a new stop unlocked;
  • automatically collected app data: an app instance identifier, first open, sessions and engagement time, device model, operating system version, language, and approximate country or region derived from the IP address.

We see these only as statistics; they do not tell us who you are. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland this is subject to the same consent choice as the ads. User-level analytics data is kept for 2 months; aggregated reports may be kept longer.

More information: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information. Information is processed by Google as described above, as our service provider and under its own policies (Google Privacy Policy). We may disclose information if required by law.

Children

Strait Control is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent at any time in Settings → Ad choices (where the consent form applies).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete everything stored on your device by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Write to ismailonder.dev@gmail.com; as we hold no data that identifies you directly, we will help you exercise these rights with Google's tools.

Security

Data is sent to Google over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we only use established services and collect no more than the game needs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
